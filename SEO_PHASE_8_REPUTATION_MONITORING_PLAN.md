## 1. Reputation Operational Benchmarks

To maintain an unassailable digital reputation, Ratiwal Dream Estates commits to the following operational performance service level agreements (SLAs):

| Reputation Metric | Target SLA / Standard | Monitoring Cadence | Escalation Threshold |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Public Google Rating** | Maintain ≥ 4.8 Stars | Weekly | Drop below 4.7 Stars |
| **Negative Review Response Time** | Within 12 Hours | Real-Time / Daily | Unanswered > 24 Hours |
| **Positive Review Response Time** | Within 48 Hours | Weekly | Unanswered > 7 Days |
| **Review Response Rate** | 100% of public reviews answered | Monthly | Response rate < 95% |
| **Internal Feedback CSAT** | ≥ 92% Positive on Site Visits | Monthly | CSAT < 88% |
| **Complaint Resolution Window** | Resolved within 48 Hours | Weekly | Open complaints > 72h |

## 2. Multi-Tier Escalation Matrix

```
LEVEL 1: GENERAL INQUIRY / TIMING DELAY
└── Handled by: Advisory Coordinator / Support Desk
    Action: Send apology, provide updated timeline, share requested plot maps.

LEVEL 2: DOCUMENTATION OR PRICING CLARIFICATION
└── Handled by: Legal Operations Lead / Senior Due Diligence Analyst
    Action: Pull certified 90-A revenue extracts, clarify development charges, schedule phone call.

LEVEL 3: FORMAL GRIEVANCE / NEGATIVE PUBLIC REVIEW / DISPUTE
└── Handled by: Suresh Kumawat (Principal Advisor & Founder)
    Action: Direct telephone outreach from leadership, internal audit of advisor notes, personal resolution.
```

## 3. Recurring Monitoring Cadence

### Daily Checklist (5 Minutes):
- Check Google Business Profile mobile app/portal for new review alerts.
- Check WhatsApp Business inbox for escalated client dissatisfaction.

### Weekly Checklist (30 Minutes):
- Review Google Business Profile ratings and unanswered comments.
- Audit public social media post comments for negative inquiries.
- Verify that zero unanswered negative reviews remain older than 12 hours.

### Monthly Review (Integrated with Phase 4 Monthly SEO Report):
- Calculate total new verified reviews acquired during the month.
- Audit complaint category breakdown to identify operational bottlenecks (e.g. if 3 clients noted delays in receiving Khasra maps).
- Review and archive internal feedback submissions.
