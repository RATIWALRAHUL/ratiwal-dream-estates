## 2. Rules (non-negotiable)
Never: buy links in bulk, use private blog networks, run automated directory submissions, create fake author/expert profiles, swap irrelevant links, use keyword-stuffed anchors, or manufacture testimonials or press coverage. Anchors should be the brand name or a natural phrase. Everything here is outreach **proposed for owner approval**; nothing is sent automatically.

## 4. Vetting checklist for any candidate (score before any outreach)
1. **Editorial relevance:** does the site cover Indian/Rajasthan property topics for real readers?
2. **Legitimacy:** real contact details, named editors, history, no link-selling pages.
3. **Audience relevance:** would the firm's buyers read it?
4. **Editorial standards:** published guidelines; paid content labelled; contributor review.
5. **Link quality:** link would be editorial and in context; `nofollow`/sponsored labels respected (not a failure).
6. **Reputation risk:** any adverse press, spam signals, or association with unlicensed brokers?
Reject on any "no" for 2, 4 or 6.

## 5. Proposed programme (sequence, not dates)

| Step | Action | Needs approval |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fix the trust gaps and finish the legal review (prerequisites) | yes (content/data changes) |
| 2 | Build 2 linkable assets: (a) "Plot document verification checklist" (PDF + page), (b) "How to check a RERA project" walkthrough with dated screenshots | yes (publication) |
| 3 | Ask the three developers for factual listing pages naming the firm as an authorised advisor (written authorisation first) | yes (outreach) |
| 4 | Prepare an advisor **expert profile** (real bio, verifiable registration) and 3–4 quote-ready, factual commentary points for local journalists | yes (outreach) |
| 5 | Vet 5–10 candidate outlets/associations with §4; shortlist ≤ 3 | owner reviews list |
| 6 | Manual outreach, one-to-one, owner-approved copy; log every contact | yes per message |
| 7 | Track mentions, referral traffic and brand searches in Search Console / GA4 once connected | needs connected analytics |

Sustainable pace: a handful of genuine mentions per quarter beats volume. No target number is promised.

## 6. Measurement (once data exists)
Brand-query impressions and clicks (Search Console), referral sessions and inquiries by source (GA4 — not installed), count of new referring domains (needs a backlink tool; unavailable now). Each mention is logged with URL, date, type, whether a link exists, and whether it is editorial.

## 7. Risks and dependencies
- Outreach before fixing trust gaps (above).
- Any link tied to payment or exchange violates Google's link policies (the link-spam policy page was not re-fetched in this phase; confirm wording before outreach templates are written).
- Dependencies: owner approval, developer cooperation, legal review, GA4 for referral/inquiry measurement.
