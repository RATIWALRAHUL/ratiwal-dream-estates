## 7. Keyword Implementation Standards

1. **Title Tag Rule:** Never keyword-stuff. Primary keyword must appear in the first 40 characters.
2. **Anchor Text Rule:** Use natural descriptive phrases (e.g., *"explore JDA approved plots in Vatika"*) rather than exact repetitive anchors (*"plots in jaipur"*).
3. **Cannibalization Prevention:**
   - Homepage targets **consultancy & advisory** (*"Real Estate Consultant in Jaipur"*).
   - `/properties` targets **broad catalog** (*"Plots for Sale in Jaipur"*).
   - Dedicated corridor pages target **micro-market plots** (*"Plots on Ajmer Road"*).
   - Articles target **how-to and legal verification** (*"How to verify JDA land papers"*).
