## Executive Summary

Search engine algorithms reward websites with clear, non-overlapping search intent mapping. When multiple pages on a website compete for the same primary keyword (keyword cannibalization), rankings fluctuate, PageRank is diluted, and search engines struggle to select the canonical destination.

This document establishes an authoritative **one-keyword, one-canonical-URL** mapping across all existing public pages and proposed content cluster topics.

## 2. Anti-Cannibalization Governance Rules

To preserve search equity and eliminate internal ranking conflicts:
1. **The Broad Keyword Rule:**  
   - `plots in jaipur` belongs exclusively to `/locations/jaipur`.
   - `residential plots for sale in jaipur` belongs exclusively to `/properties`.
   - `real estate consultant in jaipur` belongs exclusively to `/` (Homepage).
   - Under no circumstances will a blog post or property detail page target these exact primary head terms.
2. **The Project Query Rule:**  
   - Specific branded project keywords (`neelkanth nagar`, `riyasat paradise ii`, `happy business park`) belong strictly to their individual detail pages under `/properties/[slug]`.
   - Location hub cards and insight articles link to these canonical URLs but do not attempt to rank for the standalone project name.
3. **The Regulatory Query Rule:**  
   - Queries regarding legal acts and procedures (`section 90a rajasthan land revenue act`, `jda patta types`) belong strictly to educational insight guides under `/insights/[slug]`.
