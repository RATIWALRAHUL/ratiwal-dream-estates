# Phase 5 — Final Recommendations

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Scope delivered:** Stage 5A–5E *research, audit and planning*. **No website code, database record, schema, third-party profile or outreach was changed or sent.** Phase 6 has not been started and nothing will be implemented without approval.
Legend: ✅ VERIFIED · 💡 RECOMMENDATION · ⛔ UNAVAILABLE.

## 1. The five things that matter most

1. **Trust before growth.** The biggest risks to local credibility are *claims the records can't back up*, not missing keywords: testimonials/case studies with no consent evidence, an unverifiable legal reviewer, a blanket "pre-approved loans" line on all nine projects, blanket "AVAILABLE" on 67/67 plot options, and an unproven Maharashtra presence.
2. **Don't create more locations or profiles.** Three of six location pages (Bhiwadi, Navi Mumbai, Panvel) have **no inventory and no verifiable engagement evidence**; one inventory page (Kishangarh) is thin. Google's doorway policy and its Business Profile rules both argue for fewer, evidence-backed pages and **one** profile at most.
3. **The legal content needs lawyer review before more is written.** Today's research suggests the Section 90-A article's "sole mechanism" framing may be incomplete (rural vs urban rules; a 2022 amendment bill).
4. **There is no search data yet.** Search Console isn't connected and GA4 isn't installed, so no baseline, no location demand and no conversion data exist. Everything about priorities is qualitative until that changes.
5. **A few fixes are tiny and safe once approved:** hours mismatch, `#` social icons, test-data settings record, case-study image mismatch.
