## 2. Accuracy risks in existing legal content — fix BEFORE adding more legal articles

I read the Section 90-A article in detail and cross-checked its framing against today's research (all research sources are **secondary**; the primary Rajasthan government pages were not retrievable — see §3). These are **flags for legal review, not corrections**:

| # | Flag | Basis | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | The article says Section 90-A "is the **sole** statutory mechanism" for converting agricultural land. Search results indicate 90-A was historically a **rural** land-use change provision, with **urban** conversion handled under separate rules (a 1981 urban-areas rules set, rural rules of 2007), that an amendment bill touching 90-A sub-section (8) was introduced in 2022 (outcome not confirmed), and that courts have addressed regularisation under 90-A | S5: PRS Bill No. 11 of 2022 (Rajasthan); DownToEarth; LiveLaw; legitquest rules pages | **High** — an absolute claim on a legal point that may be incomplete |
| L2 | The article describes the Khatedar "surrendering" rights so land "vests" in the authority and implies this is the standard process for JDA plotted townships | needs primary-source check of the current Act and urban rules | High |
| L3 | JDA patta article: the earlier plan referenced an "official JDA Citizen Portal" for online patta verification. Today's search found JDA moving patta **issuance** online via the state SSO portal, but **no public patta-verification page** described | S5: Pink City Post; guides | Medium–High |
| L4 | "Four patta categories" and "99-year lease" style statements need an official JDA source | S5 (broker guides only) | Medium |

## 4. Editorial standards (apply to every article)
- Named author with a real, verifiable bio; named legal reviewer **only with proof of credentials and consent**; published and last-reviewed dates; a "Sources" block with URLs and access dates.
- Plain-language disclaimer: "Information for general guidance, not legal or investment advice. Rules change; verify with the authority or a qualified lawyer."
- No returns, appreciation, or "safe investment" claims; no price figures without an official source and date.
- Each article links to at least one relevant location/project page **only where a genuine inventory link exists**, and to related guides.
- No AI-generated imagery (project rule); no stock photos.
