## 2. Platform-by-Platform Findings & Risk Analysis

### A. Google Business Profile (GBP) — The Critical Public Reputation Anchor
- **Significance:** In local real estate, Google Business Profile is the first asset a prospective plot buyer sees when searching "Ratiwal Dream Estates Jaipur" or local plot keywords in Google Maps.
- **Current Observation:** The business has local brand equity, but public review collection has not been systematically integrated into the customer transaction lifecycle.
- **Key Risk:** Without an active influx of genuine, verified client reviews, a single disgruntled prospect or competitor attack can disproportionately depress the overall star rating.

### B. Independent Real Estate Directory Citations
- **Current Observation:** Third-party aggregators (Justdial, Sulekha, Housing, TradeIndia) occasionally scrape public RERA records or business filings to create unmanaged placeholder profiles.
- **Key Risk:** These directory pages often display outdated telephone numbers or generic placeholder forms that siphon inquiries away from the official website.
- **Action Required:** Audit and claim all unmanaged profiles; align NAP with `https://www.ratiwaldreamestates.com/`.
