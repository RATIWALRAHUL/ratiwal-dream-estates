## 2. Comprehensive Findings Summary

### A. Verified Public Reputation Baseline
- **Legal Legitimacy:** High. The business operates under verified Rajasthan RERA license `RAJ/A/2019/983` with Suresh Kumawat as Principal Advisor.
- **On-Site Case Studies:** The website hosts 4 verified, consent-backed client testimonials focusing on Section 90-A land conversion, JDA layout sanction, and NRI land advisory.
- **Search Snippet Trust:** Zero self-serving review stars are injected into structured data, preserving the domain from Google manual action penalties.

### B. Visual Identity Finding: Header Logo vs. Favicon Mismatch
- **Observation:** The website header displays the **Architectural Roof + 5 Vertical Pillar Lines** emblem (`ratiwal-logo.svg`), whereas the favicon and Google search snippet display the **Geometric Hexagonal "R" Symbol** (`favicon.ico` / `icon.png`).
- **Remediation:** Regenerating `favicon.ico`, `favicon.png`, and `src/app/icon.png` from `public/favicon.svg` using `sharp` will align the Google search results icon with the official website header.

### C. Review Platform Coverage & Gaps
- **Google Business Profile:** Verified local presence, but lacks an automated post-transaction review collection mechanism.
- **Directory Citations:** Several real estate portals hold unmanaged agent profile listings that require citation claiming and NAP normalization.
- **Grievance Triage:** Dissatisfaction during physical site visits or phone inquiries is currently managed informally rather than through an auditable feedback system.
