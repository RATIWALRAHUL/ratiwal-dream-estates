# SEO Phase 2 — Route Inventory

**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Method:** Every route below was fetched from a local production build (`next build` + `next start`) and its rendered `<head>` was parsed. "Current" values are the state **before** Phase 2 (captured from the Phase 1 build); "Recommended action" states what Phase 2 did. Nothing here comes from source reading alone.
**Crawl scope:** all 37 URLs in `sitemap.xml` (every public page, property, location, article and case study), plus 9 private-route probes and 6 not-found probes.

## 1. Route families in the App Router (157 `page.tsx` files)

| Family | Pages | Indexable? | How it is kept out of / in the index |
|---|---|---|---|
| `(marketing)` | 16 | Yes | Each page calls `getMetadata({ slug })` → self-referential canonical, `index, follow`. |
| `dashboard` | 111 | **No** | `dashboard/layout.tsx` and `dashboard/(auth)/layout.tsx` set `noindex, nofollow`; middleware requires a session; `robots.txt` disallows `/dashboard`. |
| `kyc` | 1 | **No** | `kyc/submit/[token]` sets `noindex, nofollow`; token-gated; `robots.txt` disallows `/kyc`. |
| `partner` | 9 | **No** | `partner/layout.tsx` sets `noindex, nofollow`; middleware requires a partner session; `robots.txt` disallows `/partner/` and `/partner$`. |
| `payments` | 1 | **No** | `payments/pay/[token]` — `noindex, nofollow` added in Phase 1; token-gated; `robots.txt` disallows `/payments`. |
| `portal` | 18 | **No** | Every portal page exports `robots: noindex, nofollow`; middleware requires a customer session; `robots.txt` disallows `/portal`. |
| `preview` | 1 | **No** | `preview/[token]` sets `noindex, nofollow`; token-gated; `robots.txt` disallows `/preview`. |

There is no top-level `/login`, `/register` or `/account` route (login and profile pages exist only under `/portal` and `/partner`). There is no separate public "services" route; services content lives on `/about`, `/why-choose-us` and `/investment`. `/agents/[slug]/card` has no page file and returns 404. Route handlers under `/api` return no HTML and are disallowed in `robots.txt`.
