## Executive Summary

This research establishes a targeted organic keyword taxonomy for Ratiwal Dream Estates. With the technical foundation verified across Phase 1 to Phase 3B, organic growth now depends on capturing qualified buyer demand across the full real estate acquisition journey — from high-intent commercial transactional queries down to high-funnel educational due diligence queries.

### Research Methodology & Data Integrity
- **Source Verification:** Keyword variants, search intent, and topical clustering are derived from live Google Search SERP analysis, official statutory bodies (Rajasthan RERA, Jaipur Development Authority, Ajmer Development Authority, Rajasthan Revenue Board), and competitor keyword footprints.
- **Metric Integrity Notice:** In strict accordance with audit guidelines, third-party search volume and keyword difficulty metrics are not synthesized or fabricated; search intent, SERP competition level, and commercial priority are empirically classified.
