## 1. Keyword Categorization Methodology

Keywords are classified by buyer journey intent:
* **Transactional (Commercial):** Ready to buy/invest, looking for verified plots with price/size filters.
* **Commercial Investigation:** Comparing consultants, agencies, RERA advisors, and project legality.
* **Informational:** Seeking legal guidance, JDA patta checks, Section 90-A conversion, circle rates.
* **Geographic / Corridor:** Micro-market specific queries tied to arterial highway corridors.
