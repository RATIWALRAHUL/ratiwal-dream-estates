# Phase 5 — Location Growth Strategy

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Stage:** 5A/5B research only — **no pages, profiles or content were created or changed.**
Legend: ✅ VERIFIED · 💡 RECOMMENDATION · ⛔ UNAVAILABLE. Classification and evidence are detailed in the Local SEO Audit; sources S1–S7 as defined there.

## 1. Principle
Grow where there is **real inventory or provable advisory activity**. Google's spam policy (✅ S2) names "multiple... pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" as doorway abuse, so more location pages is never the strategy. The goal is fewer, better, evidence-backed pages.
